2

Client older than the gateway: Warning or Information?

Needs you New in round 2 Why you:costly to reverse src/Sync.Client/Handshake/VersionCheck.cs:47

On every connect the client compares its protocol version with the gateway's. When the client is one minor version behind, the gateway serves it in compatibility mode (sync works, using the older message format) and the client writes one log line. This PR sets that line to Warning.

Two facts make the level matter. On-prem clients upload only Warning and above to our central telemetry; Information stays in the customer's local log file. And a client reconnects every 30 seconds, so the line repeats all day:

Worked numbers, last rolloutValueSource
On-prem installs340licence report, October
Share one minor version behind during a rollout12%4.1 rollout, weeks 1 to 7
Clients behind: 340 × 0.1241
Connects per client per day: 86,400 s ÷ 30 s2,880SyncOptions.ReconnectInterval
Warning lines uploaded per day: 41 × 2,880118,080

In plain words: as the PR stands, about 118,000 identical Warning lines a day reach central telemetry during a rollout.

Why it is costly to reverse: the level ships inside the client build. Release 4.2 goes to all 340 installs; changing the level afterwards needs another client release, and the last one took 7 weeks to reach 95% of installs.
